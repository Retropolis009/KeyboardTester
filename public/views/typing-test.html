How Our Online Typing Speed Test Evaluates Keyboard Performance

A reliable typing speed test reveals more than finger dexterity—it exposes subtle switch fatigue, stabilizer rattle, and double-actuating keys under real sustained typing rhythm. When upgrading from membrane domes to linear, tactile, or Hall-effect magnetic switches, running controlled 30-second and 60-second WPM benchmarks quantifies whether lighter springs improve speed without hurting accuracy.
