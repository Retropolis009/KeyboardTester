How the Online Typing Speed Test Evaluates Keyboard Performance

A reliable typing speed test reveals more than finger dexterity — it exposes switch fatigue, stabiliser rattle, and double-actuating keys under sustained typing rhythm. When moving from membrane domes to linear, tactile, or Hall-effect magnetic switches, controlled 30-second and 60-second WPM benchmarks quantify whether lighter springs actually buy speed without costing accuracy.

Net WPM subtracts incorrect characters from the gross rate, so mash-typing cannot inflate the score. Raw cadence ignores errors entirely and reflects pure key-travel throughput, while the accuracy figure is a direct count of matched characters against the passage. Comparing all three tells you whether a slow score is a hardware latency problem or simply unfamiliar vocabulary.
