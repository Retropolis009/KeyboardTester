How to Run an Accurate Keyboard Chatter Test

To perform a reliable keyboard chatter test, type natural paragraphs for two minutes or tap suspect switches 40 to 50 times at normal cadence without holding keys into OS auto-repeat. Because our profiler ignores operating-system repeat events and measures the exact keyup-to-keydown gap using high-precision browser clocks, genuine switch contact bounce stands out immediately under 35 milliseconds.

Fixing Chattering Mechanical Switches

Once you identify which switch scan codes report bounce faults, follow our step-by-step Mechanical Switch Chatter Repair Guide, verify full matrix wiring on the Online Keyboard Tester, test cadence on the Typing Speed Test, or check mouse switches on the Mouse Tester.
