How to Diagnose Defective Mouse Microswitches & Scroll Encoders

Using a dedicated mouse tester online quickly determines whether erratic drag-and-drop releases or unintended double-clicks come from OS double-click speed settings or physical mechanical wear inside Omron, Kailh, or Huano microswitches. When internal copper leaf springs lose tension, a single finger click produces two electrical pulses under 50 milliseconds apart.

Checking Optical vs. Mechanical Scroll Encoders

Roll your mouse wheel steadily upward inside the diagnostic pad. If the downward step counter increments sporadically during upward scrolling, dust inside the rotary encoder is interrupting quadrature phase signals.
